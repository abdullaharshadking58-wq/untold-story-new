UNTOLD

Ek kahani jo kabhi poori tarah kahi nahi gayi.

Ye sirf kuch school memories nahi hain. Ye un lamhon ki kahani hai jo waqt ke saath guzarte gaye, lekin dil se kabhi nahi guzray.

02 / 08
CHAPTER ONE

2019

The Beginning

2019 ek bilkul normal sa saal tha. Humein bilkul nahi pata tha ke ye saal hamari zindagi ki ek khoobsurat memory ban jayega.

School ki routine, friends, hasi mazaak, classes aur roz ke chhote chhote lamhe... sab kuch normal tha.

Kabhi ek hi desk par baithna, baatein karna aur bina kisi wajah ke hans dena us waqt itna important nahi lagta tha.

Us waqt humein pata hi nahi tha ke hum memories bana rahe thay.

03 / 08
CHAPTER TWO

The Days We Had

2019 se 2022 tak ka waqt shayad sirf kuch saal tha, lekin un saalon mein bohat sari memories chhupi hui hain.

School sirf books aur classes ki wajah se yaad nahi rehta. School un logon ki wajah se yaad rehta hai jo us waqt hamare aas paas hotay hain.

Woh sitting, woh conversations, woh hasi mazaak, friends ka shor, school functions aur woh simple se din...

Waqt guzarta gaya. Din weeks ban gaye aur weeks months. Phir school ke woh din bhi khatam ho gaye.

Humne us waqt sirf din guzare thay... lekin waqt ne un dinon ko memories bana diya.

04 / 08
CHAPTER THREE

2022

The Last Days

2022 mein school ke woh aakhri din aaye jab humein pata tha ke sab kuch change hone wala hai.

School khatam hua aur sab apni apni zindagi mein busy hotay gaye.

Jo shakhs kabhi roz nazar aata tha, woh dheere dheere ek memory ban gaya.

Kabhi dobara milne ki koshish hui, kabhi baat karne ka dil hua, lekin zindagi hamesha hamari marzi ke mutabiq nahi chalti.

Kuch log zindagi se door ho jate hain, lekin unki memories dil se door nahi hotin.

05 / 08
AFTER SCHOOL

Some Things Stayed

School ke baad waqt bohat badal gaya, lekin kuch feelings waise hi reh gayin.

Phir ek waqt aaya jab TikTok par dobara conversation hui.

Purani memories dobara yaad aayin, purani baatein dobara mehsoos hui, aur jo feelings waqt ke saath chup ho gayi thin woh phir se samne aa gayin.

Baat shuru karne ke liye jo tareeqa use hua, woh sahi nahi tha. Us ghalti ko ghalti hi kehna chahiye.

Lekin uske baad jo feelings thin, woh fake nahi thin.

Kuch feelings ko explain karna mushkil hota hai. Bas dil unhein mehsoos karta hai.

THE TRUTH

What Remains

“

Har kahani ka ending waisa nahi hota jaisa hum sochte hain.

Kabhi kabhi hum kisi ko dil se chahte hain, lekin phir bhi uski choice aur uski khushi ka respect karna padta hai.

Jo hua, woh hamari story ka hissa tha. Jo nahi hua, usay Allah ki raza samajhna bhi zaroori hai.

Kuch log hamare saath nahi rehte, lekin unki yaadein hamesha hamare saath rehti hain.

07 / 08
A LETTER

For The Person Behind The Memories

Shayad ye sab kehna zaroori nahi tha, lekin kuch baatein dil mein reh jati hain.

Main bas itna chahta hoon ke tum hamesha khush raho. Zindagi tumhein woh sab de jo tum deserve karti ho.

Jo memories humne saath banayin, woh meri life ka ek khoobsurat hissa rahengi.

Main kisi cheez ko force nahi karna chahta. Har insaan ki apni choice aur apni zindagi hoti hai.

Bas itna kehna hai...

Thank you for being a part of those beautiful days.
